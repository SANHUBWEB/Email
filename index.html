Data & privacy

This standalone version stores complaint records in your browser's local storage. It does not upload your information to a server.

Do not upload unnecessary identity documents. Never store OTPs, passwords, PINs or complete card numbers in complaint notes. Use a secure device and keep backups protected.

Gmail accounts

Checking…

Direct sending uses Google OAuth through your SANZONE backend. Gmail passwords are never asked for. Access tokens stay encrypted on the server, never in this page or browser storage.

Where is data stored? Complaints, drafts and profile stay in this browser (local). When you press Send, the email, subject and attachments go to your backend only to hand them to Gmail; they are not kept there. Setup steps are in README.
